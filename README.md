# Fogo Smart Frame 👓

### A simple, lightweight approach to everyday anti-fog eyewear

**Fogo Smart Frame** is a conceptual anti-fog spectacle frame designed
to reduce lens fogging caused by warm breath, especially when wearing
masks or working in environments where temperature and humidity change.

The concept focuses on **passive airflow guidance** rather than
electronics or batteries. A thin, comfortable airflow structure around
the nose bridge and upper frame helps redirect warm, moisture-rich air
away from the lens surface.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 🚨 The Problem

Eyeglass lenses can fog when warm, humid air from breathing reaches a
cooler lens surface.

This can happen during:

-   😷 Mask usage
-   🏥 Healthcare and laboratory work
-   🏗️ Construction and protective-wear environments
-   🏍️ Riding and outdoor activities
-   🍲 Exposure to warm food or steam
-   ❄️ Moving between warm and air-conditioned environments

Frequent fogging can interrupt clear vision and cause discomfort.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 💡 The Fogo Concept

Fogo Smart Frame is designed around a simple idea:

> **Guide warm breath away from the lens before it causes significant
> fogging.**

The frame concept uses:

-   A **thin transparent silicone airflow guide** near the nose bridge
-   **Micro airflow channels** integrated along the upper frame area
-   Side openings that allow redirected warm air to escape
-   A lightweight structure designed to work with everyday prescription
    glasses

The system is intended to work **passively**, without batteries, motors,
or electronic components.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## ⚙️ How It Works

### 1. Warm breath is generated

When a person exhales, warm and humid air moves upward from the nose and
mouth area.

### 2. Air is guided

The nose-bridge airflow guide helps direct the warm airflow away from
the lens surface.

### 3. Air moves through the channels

The frame's thin micro-channel structure provides a guided path toward
the sides.

### 4. Warm air exits

The airflow is redirected through the side openings instead of
concentrating directly on the lens.

### 5. Clearer viewing

Reducing direct exposure of the lens to warm humid breath can help
reduce fog formation.

> **Note:** Fogo is a conceptual prototype/design solution. Its
> real-world anti-fog performance would need controlled testing under
> different temperature, humidity, breathing, and environmental
> conditions.

## 🧠 Design Approach

The project focuses on solving the fogging problem through **physical
airflow management** rather than relying on continuous electronic
control.

This approach aims to keep the frame:

**Simple • Lightweight • Reusable • Battery-free • Practical**

AI or sensors could potentially be explored in future versions for
environmental monitoring or adaptive airflow control, but they are **not
required for the core passive concept**.
